## NightWatch
A discord bot that allows you to have seamless management for your roleplay server. It has the basic barebones and will have fully-fledged custom integrations. 

### Demo: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9GHtquILcJk

***
### Testing on a Server
Once you have finished reading through this guide to each command, join the testing server to use them!

### Moderation
> **.ban [user*] [duration ? indefinite] [reason ? No reason provided.]**
> - Bans a user from the guild.
> **.unban [user_id*] [reason ? No reason provided.]**
> - Removes a ban from a user.
> 
> **.kick [user*] [reason ? No reason provided.]**
> - Kicks a user from the guild.
> 
> **.timeout [user\*] [duration*] [reason ? No reason provided.]**
> - Allows you to mute/timeout a member of the guild.
> **.untimeout [user*] [reason ? No reason provided.]**
> - Removes the timeout/mute from a user.
> 
> **.warn [user*] [reason\*]**
> - Warns a user: sends them a DM and logs the warning.
> 
> **/moderation history [victim ? null] [agent ? null]**
> - Allows you to see the previous moderations received by a user or sent by a user.
> 
> **/moderation manage [id\*] [action*] **
> - Allows you to manage a moderation: viewing, editing and deleting.

### Sessions

> **/sessions boost**

> **/sessions start**
> **/sessions vote**
> - All send the relevant embed that has been set in the **/setup** command.## Command Usage:

### Staff
> **/staff infract [user*] [type\*] [reason \*]**
> - Infract a user (sends an embed to the specified channel.
> 
> **/staff promote [user*] [new\*] [old\*] [reason\*]**
> - Promote a user (sends an embed).## Command Usage:

### Tickets
> **/tickets changepanel [panel*]**
> - Move a currently open ticket to a different panel.
> - Applies role and category changes.
> 
> **/tickets embed**
> - Send the ticket embed where people can open tickets.
> 
> **/tickets rename [name*]**
> - Renames the ticket (renames the channel).## Command Usage:

### Utility
> **.ping**
> - Shows information about the bots current status.
> 
> **/utility setup**
> - Sends an embed where you can configure the bot's settings.
